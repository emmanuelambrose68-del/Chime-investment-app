About Chime

Learn how the Chime investment model works.

💡

What is Chime?

Chime Investment is designed to provide structured investment opportunities while allowing users to monitor their capital, returns and investment cycles.

📈

How It Works

Users choose an investment plan within the available capital range. Each investment operates on a 7-day cycle.

💵

25% Return

The Chime investment model provides a stated 25% return on investment capital at the end of each completed 7-day cycle, according to the applicable investment terms.

🔄

Capital Reinvestment

The original investment capital remains allocated for the next investment cycle unless the user chooses to submit a separate capital withdrawal request.

⏱️

7-Day Timer

Once an investment starts, a live countdown displays the remaining time until the current investment cycle reaches its scheduled completion.

🧠

Invest Wisely

  • Understand the investment terms before investing.
  • Consider your financial goals.
  • Keep records of your investment activity.
  • Understand how withdrawals and reinvestment work.
