About Chime

Learn how the Chime investment model works.

💡

What is Chime?

Chime Investment is designed to provide structured investment opportunities while allowing users to monitor their capital, returns and investment cycles.

📈

How It Works

Users choose an investment plan within the available capital range. Each investment plan has its own cycle based on the investment level: Starter is 7 days, Basic and Growth are 5 days, while Pro and Elite are 2 days.

💵

Target Return

Investment plans display their stated target return and applicable investment terms.

⏱️

Investment Cycle Timer

Once an investment starts, a live countdown displays the remaining time until the current investment cycle reaches its scheduled completion.

🧠

Invest Wisely

  • Understand the investment terms before investing.
  • Consider your financial goals.
  • Keep records of your investment activity.
  • Understand how deposits, withdrawals and reinvestment operate.
