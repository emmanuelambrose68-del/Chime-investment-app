About Chime

Learn how the Chime investment model is designed to work.

💡

What is Chime?

Chime Investment is a platform designed to give users access to structured investment opportunities while allowing them to monitor their capital, investment cycles and account activity.

📈

How the 7-Day Cycle Works

Each investment operates on a 7-day cycle. After an investment is created, the platform displays a countdown showing the remaining time until the cycle reaches its scheduled completion.

💵

Return Structure

The Chime investment model uses a stated target return of 25% of the investment capital per 7-day cycle. Returns and investment capital are tracked separately within the investment record.

🔄

Reinvestment

At the end of an investment cycle, capital may continue into another investment cycle according to the applicable investment terms. Users who want their capital returned should submit a separate withdrawal request.

🧠

Invest Wisely

  • Understand an investment before committing funds.
  • Never invest money you cannot afford to lose.
  • Consider your financial goals and risk tolerance.
  • Avoid making investment decisions because of pressure from others.
  • Keep records of your investments and transactions.
Risk Disclosure

Investments involve risk. The stated 25% target return is presented as part of the Chime investment model. Actual investment performance can depend on the underlying business and applicable terms.
