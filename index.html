Learn About Chime

Understand how the Chime Investment model works.

💰

Investment Model

Chime Investment allows users to select an investment plan based on their available capital.

📈

Target Return

The investment plans displayed in the app show a target return of 25% for the selected cycle.

⏱️

Investment Cycle

Each plan has its own investment cycle. The dashboard timer shows the remaining time after an investment is started.

🔄

Reinvestment

According to the Chime model, the remaining capital can be held for reinvestment into the next investment cycle.

💵

Capital Withdrawal

Users who want to withdraw capital can submit a separate withdrawal request according to the applicable procedures.

Important

Investment returns and processing depend on the actual operation of the Chime Investment service. Review the applicable terms before investing.
